Country Acres Mobile Home Park — Three-Parcel Assemblage — Broker Price Opinion

Three contiguous parcels totaling 58.81 acres (legal description) in the Anderson Creek Township of unincorporated Harnett County, owned of record by the Mitzi D. Allison Revocable Trust. The main 41.67-acre parcel at 4473 Ray Rd is the Country Acres MHP, carrying 20 county-billed residential units and 26 addressed mobile-home sites. The two companion parcels on Brandi Ln (12.00 ac and 5.14 ac) are largely wooded. All three are zoned RA-20M. Subject property summary

Location
4473 Ray Rd and Brandi Ln, Spring Lake, NC 28390 (Spring Lake mailing address; unincorporated Harnett County, Anderson Creek Township). The site sits in the Anderson Creek growth area northwest of Spring Lake, which serves Fort Bragg housing demand. It borders Ford Springs Baptist Church and has frontage on Ray Rd.
Owner of record
Mitzi D. Allison, Trustee, and the Mitzi D. Allison Revocable Trust, 7809 Blue Heron Dr W, Apt 2, Wilmington, NC 28411. All three parcels share one deed (Book 4160, Page 2505, recorded 7/2022). The deed shows no sale price and appears to be a transfer into the trust, not an arm's-length sale.
Park operation
County records name the main parcel "Country Acres MHP" (development type MHP; card note "Mobile Home Park"). The 2026 tax bill includes a $1,700 solid-waste fee, which equals 20 billed units at $85 each. County address points show 26 mobile-home addresses on Country Acres Dr and Sonny Dee Dr, plus one single-family address, two outbuildings and the 4473 Ray Rd office/main address. The gap between 26 addressed sites and 20 billed units suggests up to six vacant or inactive pads. That gap is unverified.
Site improvements
The county carries $163,660 of site improvements (roads, pads, utility hookups and similar) on the main parcel, about $8,183 per billed unit. Mobile homes are not valued as real property on the parcel, so this report assumes the homes are tenant-owned or separately listed personal property. They are excluded from this value.
Physical / environmental
Mostly Blaney and Gilead loamy sands, which are well drained and typical of the Sandhills. About 7.3 ac of Roanoke loam (occasionally flooded) and about 1.9 ac of open water (a pond) sit on the main parcel. The county flags wetlands on the main parcel and rates flood risk as minimal elsewhere. The 12-ac Brandi Ln parcel is forested with no wetland flag. The 5.14-ac parcel at 621 Brandi Ln is partly cleared and has one mobile home.
Service providers
Electric: South River EMC. Telephone: CenturyLink. Fire: Anderson Creek Fire District. Schools: Overhills district. 2026 tax rates: county $0.63 and fire $0.11 per $100.

Utilities & access

  • Water: Harnett County water mains run along Ray Rd, with county hydrants at 4470 Ray Rd (across from the park entrance) and 4600 Ray Rd. Whether every pad is on county water, or some are on wells, has not been confirmed.
  • Sewer: County GIS shows no county sewer line inside the subject parcels, so this report assumes septic. County gravity sewer and Pump Station 72 serve the neighboring Anderson Creek Club / Anderson Creek Crossing subdivisions. That is a long-term redevelopment consideration, not an in-place benefit.
  • Access: Park entrance from Ray Rd via the internal roads Country Acres Dr and Sonny Dee Dr. The Brandi Ln parcels are reached from Brandi Ln. The main parcel record notes an ingress/egress easement.

Zoning

All three parcels are zoned RA-20M, Harnett County's residential/agricultural district that permits manufactured homes. The park is an established, county-recognized MHP. Adding pads, filling the roughly six inactive addressed sites, or expanding onto the Brandi Ln acreage would likely need county manufactured-home-park approval and Environmental Health septic permits. A buyer should confirm both with Harnett County Development Services before counting on any expansion. The parcels also fall in a voluntary agricultural district.

Source: Harnett County Planning — Zoning GIS.

Methodology and verification basis

The comps for a small park on 58.81 acres are mostly parks on far less land, so applying a per-unit price to the whole assemblage would leave the surplus acreage unpriced. This BPO therefore values the assemblage in two pieces and reconciles them to one combined value:

  1. Operating park: sales comparison in $ per county-billed unit, using 10 closed mobile home park sales in Harnett County (2024–2026), cross-checked with a market-rent income approach.
  2. Surplus land: sales comparison in $ per acre, using 9 closed vacant RA-20 acreage sales within about 6 miles of the subject. This covers the main-parcel acreage outside an assumed 13-acre park footprint, plus both Brandi Ln parcels.
  • All comps were independently researched from Harnett County's recorded deed and sale data. The client supplied only parcel-app screenshots and tax notices, which describe the subject alone.
  • Multi-parcel sales were grouped by deed book and page, so each comp's price, acreage and unit count reflect the whole transaction.
  • Unit counts for every comp and for the subject use the same yardstick: the county solid-waste fee count, which tracks billed occupied dwellings. It does not count every possible pad.
  • All adjustments are Grant-Murray Real Estate's professional judgment as prospective listing broker. This is a broker's opinion of value, not a certified USPAP appraisal.

The low end of the range uses $32,000 per unit and $7,000 per usable acre ($910,070). The high end uses $38,000 per unit and $9,000 per usable acre ($1,103,290). The combined opinion of $1,000,000 works out to about $50,000 per billed unit including all land, or about $17,000 per acre across 58.81 acres. Grant-Murray recommends listing at $1,050,000, about 5% above the opinion. That leaves room to negotiate and reflects the upside a buyer may see in the inactive sites and surplus land.

Counter-evidence & upside

  • The county's 2026 value is higher. The 2026 reappraisal puts the three parcels at $1,243,690, about 24% above this opinion and up from $800,830 in 2025. County mass-appraisal values are not market sales and are not relied on here, but a seller may anchor to that figure.
  • Possible inactive pads. There are 26 addressed mobile-home sites against 20 billed units. If up to six pads can be re-leased without new permits, a buyer could add roughly $30,000 per year in lot rent at stabilization. Value is not credited here until that is verified.
  • Higher-priced comps exist. Five of the ten park sales closed above $44,000 per unit before adjustment. If the subject's homes are park-owned, or rents are well above $425, the park component could support $40,000+ per unit, about $100,000 more.
  • Land near Ray Rd has sold higher. The 10-acre Ray Rd sale (L1) at $14,000 per acre shows what frontage land in this corridor brings. If part of the Brandi Ln acreage could be split off with its own frontage, it could beat the $8,000 per acre used here.

Limitations

  1. No rent roll, operating statement or occupancy report was provided. Unit count relies on county solid-waste billing (20) and address points (26). Lot rent, vacancy, expenses and cap rate are market assumptions.
  2. Home ownership is assumed. The value assumes the mobile homes are tenant-owned or separate personal property and are not included. Any park-owned homes would add value and should be inventoried.
  3. Utilities are not confirmed. County water is on Ray Rd, but pad-level water source and the number, age and condition of septic systems are unverified. Septic failures could reduce value materially.
  4. The 13-acre park footprint and 9.2 constrained acres are estimates based on county soils data and aerial imagery, not a survey or wetland delineation. The GIS area (64.67 ac) is larger than the legal acreage (58.81 ac) used here.
  5. Comp details come from county recorded-sale data. Prices, acreage and billed-unit counts were not confirmed with buyers or sellers. Sale terms, home inclusion and seller financing for each comp are unknown.
  6. No site inspection, title search or environmental review was performed for this opinion.
  7. This is a broker's opinion of value for marketing purposes, prepared by Grant-Murray Real Estate, LLC. It is not a certified USPAP appraisal.
